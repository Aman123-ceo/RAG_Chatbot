1. WHAT IS DOPPLER EFFECT?
The Doppler Effect is the change in frequency of a wave when the source and observer move relative to each other.
When a vehicle moves TOWARDS the radar, the reflected frequency INCREASES.
When a vehicle moves AWAY from the radar, the reflected frequency DECREASES.

2. DOPPLER FORMULA
f' = f * (c + vr) / (c - vs)
Where:
f = original transmitted frequency
f' = observed / reflected frequency
c = speed of wave (speed of light / sound)
vr = velocity of receiver / observer
vs = velocity of source

3. HOW SPEED RADAR WORKS
- Radar transmits radio waves towards a moving vehicle.
- Waves reflect back from the vehicle.
- Because vehicle is moving, reflected frequency is shifted (Doppler shift).
- Radar measures this frequency shift.
- Bigger shift = faster vehicle. Smaller shift = slower vehicle.
- System calculates speed and displays it.

4. APPLICATIONS OF DOPPLER EFFECT
- Traffic police speed guns
- Weather radar (rain, storm tracking)
- Medical ultrasound (blood flow)
- Astronomy (stars moving towards / away)
- Aircraft navigation
